How to Install Peacock on Apple TV
==================================

If the Peacock app is not already installed:

1. Turn on your Apple TV.
2. Open the **App Store**.
3. Search for **Peacock**.
4. Select the official Peacock app.
5. Choose **Get** or **Download**.
6. Wait for the installation to complete.
7. Launch the Peacock app.

How to Activate Peacock on Apple TV
===================================

Follow these simple steps:

1. Open the Peacock app on your Apple TV.
2. Select **Sign In**.
3. An activation code will appear on your TV screen.
4. On another device, open a web browser.
5. Visit the official Peacock activation page.
6. Enter the activation code exactly as displayed.
7. Sign in with your Peacock account if prompted.
8. Wait while the activation is verified.
9. Your Apple TV screen will refresh automatically.
10. Start streaming your favorite content.

Alternative Sign-In Method
==========================

Some versions of the Peacock app allow you to sign in directly on Apple TV by entering your Peacock email address and password without using an activation code.

Common Apple TV Activation Problems
===================================

Activation Code Not Working
---------------------------

If your activation code isn't accepted:

* Double-check that the code was entered correctly.
* Make sure the code has not expired.
* Restart the Peacock app to generate a new activation code.
* Enter the new code promptly.

Unable to Sign In
-----------------

If you cannot access your account:

* Verify your email address.
* Confirm your password.
* Reset your password if necessary.
* Ensure your Peacock subscription is active.

Peacock App Won't Open
----------------------

If the application does not launch:

* Restart your Apple TV.
* Verify your internet connection.
* Update tvOS to the latest version.
* Remove and reinstall the Peacock app if necessary.

Apple TV Does Not Refresh
-------------------------

If your Apple TV remains on the sign-in screen:

* Wait a few moments.
* Restart the Peacock app.
* Restart your Apple TV.
* Generate a new activation code and repeat the process.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
=================================

Do I need an activation code every time I use Peacock on Apple TV?
------------------------------------------------------------------

No. An activation code is generally required only when signing in for the first time or after signing out of the Peacock app.

Can I use the same Peacock account on multiple Apple TV devices?
----------------------------------------------------------------

Yes. You can sign in to your Peacock account on multiple compatible devices, subject to the simultaneous streaming limits associated with your subscription.

Why did my activation code expire?
----------------------------------

Activation codes are temporary for security purposes. If the code expires, reopen the Peacock app to receive a new one.

Can I sign in without an activation code?
-----------------------------------------

Yes. Depending on the Peacock app version, you may be able to sign in directly using your Peacock account email address and password.

What should I do if activation still doesn't work?
--------------------------------------------------

Restart your Apple TV, verify your internet connection, generate a new activation code, and ensure you're signing in with the correct Peacock account. If the problem persists, visit the official Peacock Help Center for additional support.
